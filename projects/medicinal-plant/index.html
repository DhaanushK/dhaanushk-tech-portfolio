AT A GLANCE

Price patterns need coverage context.

This project reshapes annual e-CHARAK workbooks into a validated long-format dataset, analyzes the quality and availability of reported prices, and presents an interactive overview plus a focused trend page. It is designed to support investigation, not to claim a definitive sourcing recommendation.

01 / QUESTION

What can the reports tell us?

Which plants and markets have enough observations for careful comparison? How are reported values distributed over time? Which price changes or source notations should be investigated before they are used in a purchasing decision?

The source is the e-CHARAK Price Wise portal from India’s Ministry of AYUSH. The analysis records reporting periods and source worksheets to preserve traceability.

02 / BUILD

Preserve before interpreting.

Python scripts audit the source workbooks, standardize reporting periods, reshape the wide sheets, classify source representations, and validate the resulting analytical dataset. Original values and worksheet identifiers remain available for review. SQL separates plant records from price observations; Power BI provides plant and market filters with coverage-aware views.

PYTHONWorkbook audit, cleaning policy, validation, EDA, anomaly investigation
MYSQL / SQLNormalized plants and price-observation tables, staging and validation queries
EXCELSource workbooks and worksheet-level traceability
POWER BITwo-page dashboard for overview, trends, and observation coverage
03 / FINDINGS

Coverage is part of the result.

The cleaned analytical structure covers 110 plant identifiers and 31 markets. Many source values are missing or non-numeric; only positive numeric observations are used for price statistics where appropriate.

374,830observations retained in the analytical dataset
71,890positive numeric price records for descriptive analysis
31markets represented across the collected source workbooks

A focused Neem-Leaf analysis compares monthly medians and observation counts for Berhampur, Kolkata, and Silliguri.

04 / ENGINEERING

Uncertainty stays visible.

Blank values remain missing; undocumented notations are flagged rather than assigned guessed meanings; missing periods are not filled with invented prices. Extreme values are investigated and preserved when present in the source. Observation counts accompany comparisons to make uneven coverage easier to interpret.

The source’s exact price unit and definition are unverified, and the completeness of the full portal history has not been independently established. A lower reported price alone does not prove a better sourcing opportunity.
