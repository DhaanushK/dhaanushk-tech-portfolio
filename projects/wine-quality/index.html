AT A GLANCE

Compare price, rating, and place.

The project audits and cleans the Wine Enthusiast review dataset, investigates how listed prices relate to critic scores, and compares country, region, and variety patterns. Fifty SQL analyses and a five-page Power BI report support the exploration.

01 / QUESTION

How do reviews vary?

How do listed prices and critic ratings differ across countries, producing regions, and grape varieties? Which records combine a price at or below $30 with a rating of 90 or above? How much do sample size, duplicates, missing prices, and outliers affect the comparisons?

The source contains critic review records. It does not include direct sales, revenue, inventory, or consumer demand.

02 / BUILD

Audit, compare, contextualize.

Python scripts audit missing and duplicate records, clean categories, compare deduplication strategies, validate the final dataset, and produce EDA reports and figures. The cleaned data is analyzed in MySQL with 50 SQL queries across country, regional, price-band, rating, and variety questions. Power BI organizes global, country, regional, value, and variety views.

03 / FINDINGS

Relationships are not causes.

The deduplicated analytical dataset contains 97,851 records from 48 countries, 632 varieties, and 14,795 wineries. Listed price and critic rating show positive statistical association in this dataset.

0.4474Pearson correlation between listed price and rating
0.5987Spearman correlation between listed price and rating
8,912records priced ≤ $30 and rated ≥ 90

Both correlation measures describe association; they do not show that higher prices cause higher scores. The custom regional value score is exploratory, not an industry standard.

04 / ENGINEERING

Make the comparison fair.

Duplicates are examined before removal; missing prices remain missing rather than becoming zero; unusually high values are reviewed through sensitivity analysis. Comparisons use available priced records and documented minimum sample thresholds. Country and region results are interpreted with their representation and sample sizes in mind.

The reviews and prices are historical editorial records, not verified transactions or current price quotes. Review counts do not measure demand, and market conclusions should not be generalized beyond the represented sample.
